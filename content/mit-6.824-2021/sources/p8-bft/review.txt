5. Technical decisions and corrections incorporated
- Internal outline title remains 6.5840 2024 Lecture22; slides say Lecture21. Their
  2026-link relationship does not establish a new 2026 authorship date for all content.
- Slides11/13 and outline simplify clients as honest; paper3 explicitly protects
  correct clients despite arbitrary numbers of faulty clients. Access control and
  authorized malicious application actions remain separate.
- Slides12 Verify-ok shorthand is conditioned on the correct node's trusted key,
  not a proof that any authenticated sender is honest.
- Exact prepared proof = primary pre-prepare + 2f distinct backup prepares + request;
  committed = f+1 correct prepared; committed-local adds 2f+1 matching commits.
  Commit sender has prepared, but need not itself have committed locally.
- Both quorum intersections written explicitly; general N and q not confused with
  the minimal N=3f+1 formula. Bounds scoped to original model/guarantees.
- Missing-COMMIT counterexample distinguishes sending a prepare from collecting a
  prepared certificate; new-view cannot safely omit an already client-completed result.
- New view gathers 2f other reports plus own, chooses highest old-view proof per
  slot, uses certified checkpoint and no-op holes, and redoes normal phases. Backups
  verify the proof and selection rather than trusting the new primary's signature.
- Faulty members are not expelled by view changes; timing assumptions and rotating
  primary required for progress. No claim that eventual delivery alone or a correct
  primary immediately supplies bounded termination.
- FAQ84–93 '2f+1 good replicas have committed' corrected: at least f+1 correct
  replicas are prepared. FAQ's FLP/one-third shorthand not generalized to all models.
- Stable checkpoint changes evidence representation, not committed history. State
  content and certified digest must be connected. Lagging-correct-node transfer is
  not a protocol for cleaning compromised machines under arbitrary mobile attacks.
- MAC optimization requires protocol changes; external proof/technical report and
  dissertation unreviewed. MD5/RSA parameters retained only as historical context.
- Paper6.1 explicitly says view changes/retransmissions not yet implemented; all
  normal-case evaluation quoted as paper results, not personally reproduced results.
- Tables1–3 read together: 309% null write overhead vs 26% BFS-nr and 3% NFS-std
  Andrew totals, changed lookup semantics, non-equivalent BFS-nr stability guarantee.
- Intro's simultaneous fault wording and FAQ lifetime-fault boundary distinguished;
  proactive recovery and BASE are later external topics, not completed here.
