Important interpretation and correction decisions
- Distinguish function publish, image, execution environment and invocation. Warm invoke is not automatically a new MicroVM or full image transfer.
- Working set can change with execution/input and across the fleet; 512 KiB fetch granularity differs from guest I/O bytes and page-level overlay writes.
- Guest page cache precedes chunk L1/L2/L3; AZ L2 itself has memory and flash. Worker fetches S3 and fills cache on miss. Cache immutability plus private encrypted overlays preserves sharing.
- Dedup is content-derived chunk encryption with separately protected customer manifest; hash identity is not authorization; integrity detection is not repair/availability; salt limits sharing/blast radius rather than replacing key authorization.
- 2023 CTR vs 2026 GCM and 4-of-5 vs 4-of-6 explicitly kept apart. No claim to know whether these represent a deployment change, simplification or another explanation choice.
- Paper 15000 x 10 GiB/s ordinarily converts to ~161 TB/s or ~1.29 Pb/s, not the stated 150 Pb/s. Materials §1.2 flags source arithmetic/unit inconsistency without inventing an omitted factor. Motivation still holds.
- 6.4% is cited Slacker average, not Lambda empirical constant. 16 GiB manifest-overhead example is not a product limit. 512 KiB/512kB source-unit discrepancy retained.
- Dedup upload population/byte-weighting/access-frequency distinctions maintained. 23x and another 5x not generalized as universal 115x user savings.
- Tier service shares (~67%,32%,0.06%) and L2 conditional hit rate (99.9% median) not conflated; medians from different distributions not forced to sum/multiply exactly; minute buckets not individual function samples.
- Worker/client fetch latency, server-side GET/PUT processing, local-agent read and guest/end-user cold start are separate. Fig11 origin tail omitted; slide40 unspecified timing not labeled universal cold-start result.
- 25% request overhead relative to 4 stripe requests, not relative to one whole-chunk GET. Coding threshold not a consensus quorum or unlimited node/AZ resilience. Constant work scoped to tolerable failure path, not identical whole-system workload.
- Normal-origin-traffic amplification and Little's Law feedback separated. Author reports S3 can take uncached traffic; concern is increased duration/concurrency/new slots and working set. No claim that S3 capacity itself caused the described metastability.
- GC root closure plus salt isolation; expired access alarm is an extra guard, not proof that all later-needed data will be detected during finite observation. Full concurrent GC algorithm unavailable.
- SnapStart/warm reuse/demand disk load distinct. Uniqueness thought experiment marked as editorial reasoning; external snapshot paper protocol and present product support not invented.
- Rust historical compiler issue scoped to 1.46 implementation period; no claim of current compiler defect. Complete Listing2 not copied or offered as general empty-slice-safe API.
- No global Lambda launch year asserted; manuscript focuses on described architecture rather than unverified historical/product marketing statements.
