IMPORTANT TECHNICAL CORRECTIONS / UNRESOLVED IMPLEMENTATION DETAILS
1. Prototype Petal does not reject expired-lease writes on receipt. 15s margin is
   practical mitigation, not an arbitrary-delay correctness proof. Paper proposes
   receiving-side timestamp or lease-ID checks; not claimed implemented.
2. Class checksum/commit-marker descriptions are possible WAL designs. Paper clearly
   specifies per-512-byte log sequence numbers, sector old/new atomicity and CRC
   damage handling, not an unambiguous per-record checksum/commit layout. Do not
   convert instructor uncertainty into verified source-code behavior.
3. Shared readers can cache together; exclusive-only lecture is a simplification.
   busy->idle does not release distributed ownership. Lease is per client, associated
   with all its locks. Recovery inherits old locks and exclusively locks the log.
4. Versions are per 512B metadata block, not per workstation/global transaction.
   Reused metadata blocks remain metadata, preserving version interpretation.
5. Physical 128KB per-server circular log vs 1TB virtual aggregate reservation;
   256 log slots. Classroom ambiguous TB utterance is not actual log capacity.
6. Ordinary data not logged; fsync-related guarantees differ from coherence.
   Temp-file + rename alone is not a fully specified durable-replacement protocol.
7. Outline raw-disk bandwidth 336MB/s conflicts with listed 7*9*6=378; paper's
   measured Petal 100MB/s read, 43MB/s replicated write are used, no guessed cause.
8. FAQ's Petal internals/write absorption are partly conjectural. Backups, finer
   locks, larger testbed and lease repairs include proposals, not all built features.
9. Historical 2021 lab assignment not substituted for 2026 APIs; fixed commit stays
   513cac0aef010750fc74688da8a395974a83cf7a. No complete lab answers or test claims.
