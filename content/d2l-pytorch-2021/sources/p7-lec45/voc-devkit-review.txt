Editorial paraphrase: Semantic labels include twenty object classes and background. Segmentation training/validation counts are 1464 and 1449 before any local crop-size filtering. Class segmentation and object-instance segmentation are distinct masks. Annotation is not guaranteed pixel accurate. The boundary band, ambiguous/difficult/occluded objects and very small regions may be labelled void with index 255. Void pixels are ignored for evaluation and treated as unlabelled in training. The official per-class score is intersection over union TP/(TP+FP+FN), averaged over twenty classes plus background.
