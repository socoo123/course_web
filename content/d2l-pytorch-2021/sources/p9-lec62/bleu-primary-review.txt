Editorial mathematical review:
Standard corpus BLEU_N = BP * exp(sum_n w_n log p_n), with sum_n w_n=1. Baseline w_n=1/N. Corpus counts are first clipped sentence by sentence, then summed; n-grams do not cross sentence boundaries. Corpus BLEU is not the arithmetic mean of sentence BLEUs.
Pinned textbook 9.7 code instead multiplies p_n ** (0.5 ** n); it is an explicitly distinct teaching variant. The textbook prose describing the original paper as p_n^(1/n) is not the primary paper's baseline. Its claim that higher n receives larger weight does not match the coefficient 2^(-n) in log space.
