Editorial reading notes:
- evaluateImg matches independently for each image/category and IoU threshold, in descending score order, with special crowd/ignore/area treatment. Accumulate orders detections across images per class; it does not average per-image AP.
- Precision is enveloped from right to left, then sampled on 101 recall points. Missing recall levels remain zero; absent-GT settings stay -1 and summarize excludes them.
- Default detection summary uses all-area and maxDets100; parameters are configurable. None of these defaults establish CodaLab33573's deployed scorer version/settings or submission coordinate adapter.
- Local pedagogical example intentionally has one class, two ordinary noncrowd GT boxes, three predictions, no ignored boxes, no ties, and no truncation by max detections. Its rational expectations are paper deductions, pending main-agent finite validation, not a competition score or pycocotools execution.
