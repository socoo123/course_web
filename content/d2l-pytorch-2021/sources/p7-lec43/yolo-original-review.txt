Editorial paraphrase of audited facts:
An S by S grid assigns an object to the cell containing its center. Each cell predicts B boxes, five values per box (x,y,w,h,confidence), and one shared vector of C conditional class probabilities. Output is S by S by (5B+C). The VOC setting is S=7, B=2, C=20, thus 7 by 7 by 30 and 98 candidate boxes. Center coordinates are cell-relative; widths/heights are image-relative. Confidence is trained toward object presence times localization IoU. At inference the model predicts this score; ground truth is not available. Class-specific confidence multiplies the shared conditional class score by box confidence.
Section 2.2 assigns the highest-current-IoU predictor responsibility, uses squared-error terms with coordinate and no-object weights, and square roots of widths/heights. Section 2.3 retains NMS as postprocessing. Section 2.4 identifies constraints on nearby/small grouped objects and localization. These claims describe original YOLO, not all later YOLO versions.
