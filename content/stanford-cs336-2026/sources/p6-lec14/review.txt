Technical findings and corrections
- A score from source labels is not objective quality or calibrated truth. Distinguish language, domain, quality, toxicity, privacy and legal permission.
- The handout's “no model-based filtering” list is interpreted in quality-filter context, not as absence of language/toxicity classifiers throughout the pipeline.
- Hard thresholds and random retention are both distribution-changing choices. Two fixed per-item boolean predicates commute on immutable text; transformations, fitted thresholds and duplicate representative selection may not.
- MurmurHash equality alone is not exact string equality; the classroom grouping snippet is not represented as production collision-proof deduplication.
- Ideal MinHash collision probability equals set Jaccard under uniform random ordering. The100-hash example assertion <.01 error is not a general guarantee; notes give ideal variance J(1-J)/k.
- Band match means corresponding signature positions of A and B agree, not every hash function within one document producing the same value.
- LSH probability is1-(1-s^r)^b. Candidate retrieval is not a hard threshold. Exact candidate verification can remove false positives but cannot recover unseen false negatives. Worst-case candidate buckets can be large.
- Fixed classroom configuration b20,r450 has9000 positions and approximate transition about.993. Not interchangeable with b450,r20 or with FineWeb's75% configuration. Linked paper configuration interpretation remains in materials; no independent paper full-read claim made here.
- True-pair edges do not make a connected component a clique. Original A={1,2},B={1,2,3},C={2,3} counterexample establishes2/3,2/3,1/3.
- Epoch cap dimension corrected explicitly: e_s=p_s B/D_s<=C, equivalently p_s B<=C D_s. Fixed source line371's abbreviated token expression is not silently copied as an epoch formula.
- Simulated epoching aligns average repeat exposure, not all model dynamics or rare-domain coverage.
- Synthetic records, prompts, environments and independent tasks have different denominators. Teacher strength and distillation utility are not identical; “no answer-filtering benefit” in one classroom example is not a universal no-validation recommendation. No-execution trajectories are not certified correct patches.
