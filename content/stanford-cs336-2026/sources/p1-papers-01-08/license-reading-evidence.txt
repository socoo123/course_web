1. https://arxiv.org/licenses/nonexclusive-distrib/1.0/ redirected by the reader to https://arxiv.org/licenses/nonexclusive-distrib/1.0/license.html
Title: arXiv.org - Non-exclusive license to distribute.
The URI http://arxiv.org/licenses/nonexclusive-distrib/1.0/ records the submitter's grant to arXiv.org:
“I grant arXiv.org a perpetual, non-exclusive license to distribute this article.”
“I certify that I have the right to grant this license.”
“I understand that submissions cannot be completely removed once accepted.”
“I understand that arXiv.org reserves the right to reclassify or reject any submission.”
Interpretation for this audit: this is a grant to arXiv, not a public translation/adaptation license. Choose independent close reading for paper01, paper03, paper04, paper05, paper06. Attention v7 additionally grants attributed reproduction of tables and figures solely in journalistic or scholarly works on PDF p.1; that narrow grant is not permission to translate its full text.

2. https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/4.0/legalcode.en
Title: Attribution-NonCommercial-NoDerivatives 4.0 International.
Section 1(1), excerpt: Adapted Material includes material in which “the Licensed Material is translated, altered, arranged, transformed, or otherwise modified in a manner requiring permission”.
Section 2(a)(1)(A): “reproduce and Share the Licensed Material, in whole or in part, for NonCommercial purposes only”.
Section 2(a)(1)(B): “produce and reproduce, but not Share, Adapted Material for NonCommercial purposes only”.
Section 3(a)(1), explicit clarification: “For the avoidance of doubt, You do not have permission under this Public License to Share Adapted Material.”
The official deed https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/4.0/ also states attribution, noncommercial use, and no distribution of modified material.
Interpretation for this audit: RoFormer v5 is CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 per its own arXiv license link; do not publish a translated adaptation under this license. Choose independent close reading, not a full translated or paraphrased replacement.

3. https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.en
Title: Attribution 4.0 International.
Section 1(1), excerpt: Adapted Material includes material in which “the Licensed Material is translated, altered, arranged, transformed, or otherwise modified in a manner requiring permission”.
Section 2(a)(1)(A): “reproduce and Share the Licensed Material, in whole or in part”.
Section 2(a)(1)(B): “produce, reproduce, and Share Adapted Material”.
Section 3(a)(1) requires retaining creator identification, supplied copyright/license/disclaimer notices and a source URI; indicating modifications and prior modifications; and including the license text or URI.
Section 2(a)(6): no endorsement or official status is granted.
Section 2(b): moral/personality rights, patents and trademarks are not generally licensed.
The official deed https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ states that sharing and adapting, even commercially, are permitted subject to attribution, a license link and change indication.
Interpretation for this audit: paper07 PagedAttention v1 and paper08 FineWeb v2 each independently link CC BY 4.0 on arXiv; a future attributed Chinese translation of the paper is permitted within the licensor's rights. Preserve separate third-party notices. The FineWeb paper license does not establish a license to all underlying web-corpus content.
